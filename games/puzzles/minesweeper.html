📖 How to play

first click safe
  • Left‑click a cell to reveal it. If you reveal a mine → 💥 game over.
  • Right‑click (or long‑press) to flag a suspected mine 🚩.
  • Numbers show how many mines are adjacent (8 neighbours).
  • Reveal all safe cells to win. Flags help you track mines.
  • Click the ↻ button to restart anytime.

💡 First click is always safe – no mine!

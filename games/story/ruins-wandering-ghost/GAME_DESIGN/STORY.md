# Core Narrative & Story Design

> **Game**: *Ruins & the Wandering Ghost*  
> **Module**: `src/story/chapters/Chapter1.js`  

---

## Narrative Premise

You awaken under a sky full of unfamiliar stars as a wandering spirit. You have no name, no past, and no physical form—only a gentle glow and a pulling sensation toward ancient stone ruins. As you walk through the abandoned settlement, interacting with forgotten objects and revealing spectral echoes through your **Memory Sense**, the story of a lost community and your own forgotten identity begins to unfold.

---

## Chapter Structure

### Chapter 1: Awaken
- **Theme**: Discovery & Returning
- **Setting**: `REG_001_FORGOTTEN_SETTLEMENT`
- **Goal**: Retrace the 10 narrative beats to uncover why you returned.

#### Key Beat Milestones
1. **The Archway**: Awakening at the border of the ruins.
2. **The Forgotten Path**: Finding the rusted pendant under the dead trees.
3. **The Empty House**: Uncovering the carved name on the stone hearth.
4. **The Well**: Looking into the pitch-black water and hearing the fallen bucket.
5. **The Stone Garden**: Finding the stone flower at the broken fountain.
6. **The Statue**: Activating Memory Sense to reveal the statue's echo.
7. **The Broken Tower**: Reading the carved warning on the collapsed rubble.
8. **The Silent Bell**: Tolling the bell in the spectral memory realm.
9. **The Old Road**: Following the road marker toward the boundary.
10. **The Grave**: Standing at the solitary headstone—realizing your return is only the beginning.

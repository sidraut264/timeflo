## Character & NPC Profiles

### `CHAR_001_GHOST`
- **Name**: Wandering Ghost
- **Role**: Protagonist
- **Visual Description**: A translucent glowing orb of pale blue and white light surrounded by orbiting soul particles.
- **Lore**: A spirit who has returned to the sunken settlement without memories of their past life. Driven by an undeniable instinct to retrace the 10 narrative beats.

---

### `NPC_001_CHILD`
- **Name**: Lost Child
- **Role**: Memory NPC / Echo
- **Location**: `LOC_005_STONE_GARDEN`
- **Lore**: A spectral child who once played near the fountain before the settlement fell.

---

### `NPC_002_WOMAN`
- **Name**: Weaving Woman
- **Role**: Memory NPC / Kinswoman
- **Location**: `LOC_003_EMPTY_HOUSE`
- **Lore**: An elder who lived in the empty house, carving names into stone to preserve loved ones.

---

### `NPC_003_WANDERER`
- **Name**: The Stranger
- **Role**: Wayfarer / Messenger
- **Location**: `LOC_009_OLD_ROAD`
- **Lore**: A traveler who left the village when the waters rose, carving stone road markers.
